# OGMA Labs

Landing Page Prompt – OGMA

Create a premium, world-class landing page for OGMA, a technology company that helps businesses evolve through software engineering, artificial intelligence, cybersecurity, design, automation and digital strategy.

This is not a marketing agency website. It should feel like the homepage of a modern technology company such as Stripe, Linear, Notion, Vercel or Anthropic: elegant, minimalistic, purposeful and human.

Brand

Company Name:
OGMA

Tagline:
"Das ideias nascem os mundos." (From ideas, worlds are born.)

Brand Philosophy

The website should communicate that technology is never the end goal.

Technology exists to empower people.

Every company begins with an idea. Some become businesses. Others change communities. The role of OGMA is to remove technological barriers so those ideas can grow.

Avoid generic AI buzzwords like:

Digital Transformation

Innovation

Next Generation

Cutting-edge

Instead, focus on:

Human impact

Knowledge

Growth

Possibility

Trust

Long-term partnership

Building the future

The copy should feel emotional, intelligent and inspiring—not salesy.

Color Palette

Primary:
Deep Petroleum Blue (#0D1F2D)

Secondary:
Copper (#C56A2D)

Support Colors:
Graphite (#1A1F24)
Ivory (#F4F1EA)
Warm Gray (#A7ADB5)

The design should use lots of whitespace with subtle gradients and soft shadows.

Avoid flashy neon colors.

Typography

Elegant modern typography.

Large headlines.

Generous spacing.

Premium feel.

Minimal animations.

Sections

1. Hero

Large cinematic hero.

Headline should inspire ambition instead of selling services.

Example direction:

Every great company begins with an idea.

We help build everything that comes after.

CTA:
Let's Build Together

Background should feel modern and sophisticated, using abstract geometric shapes or subtle glowing lines instead of cliché technology imagery.

2. Who We Are

Tell the story of OGMA.

Explain the origin of the name (inspired by the Celtic guardian of knowledge and communication).

Present the belief that knowledge only becomes valuable when it creates real change.

3. Services

Display services as premium cards.

Include:

Software Engineering

Artificial Intelligence

Business Automation

Cybersecurity

UI/UX Design

Brand Identity

Digital Strategy

Social Media

Cloud Infrastructure

Each card should focus on business outcomes rather than technical features.

4. Why OGMA

Instead of listing features, explain principles:

Knowledge before technology.

People before processes.

Security before scale.

Design with purpose.

Long-term partnership.

5. Success Stories

Create three realistic case studies instead of generic testimonials.

Each story should describe:

the client's challenge,

the transformation,

the impact on the people behind the business.

6. Final Manifesto

Create a visually impactful full-width section.

Example tone:

"Technology is only the tool.

People are always the purpose.

Because...

From ideas, worlds are born."

7. Contact

Modern contact section with:

Phone

WhatsApp

Email

LinkedIn

Instagram

Contact Form

Large CTA encouraging visitors to start a conversation instead of requesting a quote.

Footer

Minimalist.

Include logo, navigation, copyright and social media.

UX

Responsive Mobile First

Accessibility (WCAG AA)

SEO Optimized

Fast Loading

Smooth scrolling

Subtle premium animations

Sticky transparent navigation

Modern hover effects

Overall Feeling

The visitor should leave thinking:

"This company doesn't just build software.

They understand why my business exists."

The website should feel timeless, premium, trustworthy and emotionally intelligent.
